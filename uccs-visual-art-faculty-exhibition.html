UCCS Visual Art Faculty Exhibition

GOCA presents the 2026 UCCS Visual Art Faculty Exhibition. Brand new artworks from ten distinguished faculty will be on view in the Marie Walsh Sharpe Gallery at the Ent Center for the Arts. The artists, whose works have been recognized in galleries and museums across the globe, guide the next generation of creatives in the classroom as they continue to expand their own craft. Come check out the artistic practices informing future artists in Colorado Springs.

Exhibition on View: November 12, 2026 - January 16, 2027 Marie Walsh Sharpe Gallery | Ent Center for the Arts

Opening: Thursday, November 12, 2026, 5-7p.m.
