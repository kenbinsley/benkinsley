Book Review:
Contemporary Absurdities, Existential Crises, and Visual Art
Edited by Katherine Guinness and Charlotte Kent, Bristol, Intellect Books, 2024
by Emma Sullivan in "Comedy Studies"

The title, Contemporary Absurdities, Existential Crises, and Visual Art, couched in familiar academic tripartite, suggests a more conventional book than the one we find within its covers. In the collection’s tracking of the absurd, heterogeneity has been encouraged and the usual academic forms which work to unify and effect coherence are frequently discarded, allowing for a fuller sense of encountering very different minds and approaches. The commitment to the overturning of convention is also reflected in the structure of the edition: ordered by the contributors’ first names rather than the customary surname, and with the Janks Archive of insult humour seeded throughout, ready to ambush the reader; ‘YOU HAVE A FACE LIKE A SLAPPED ARSE’ shouts one page (226).
