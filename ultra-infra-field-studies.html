ULTRA INFRA [field studies]

7" Vinyl Record (2026)
45rpm | edition of 50

ULTRA INFRA [field studies] is a numbered limited edition 7” vinyl record featuring two tracks exploring sound beyond human hearing range. Side A (Above) is a composition of ultrasonic bat echolocations. Side B (Below) features low frequency ground vibrations recorded at the root collars of various species of Colorado trees at ecozones ranging from riparian to alpine. These two tracks are only available on vinyl.

Side A: Above (6:14)
Ultrasonic bat echolocations recorded using an AudioMoth full-spectrum acoustic logger at a sample rate of 250kHz (capturing ultrasonic sound up to 125 kHz), in summer 2025.

Side B: Below (4:23)
Ground vibrations recorded at the root collars of various species of Colorado trees at ecozones ranging from riparian (lower elevation, near a water source) to alpine (near tree line ~10,800 ft) in summer 2026.

The infrasonic range (below human hearing, <20 Hz) was isolated and transposed to make low frequency vibrations audible for human listeners. While the exact sources of the sounds remain speculative, environmental factors such as seismic activity, wind through branches and leaves, xylem flow, insects, and nearby foot, car, and air traffic could be responsible for the captured vibrations.

Recorded using a LOM Geofón and transposed between 12-48 semitones (1-4 octaves).
