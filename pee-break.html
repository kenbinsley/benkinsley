Pee Break

Video (2026)

While taking a pee break in the forest, a forager discovers the rare and elusive Microglossum viride, the ‘green earth tongue.’ An encounter between two larger-than-life, sporulating ascomycetes and an amateur mycologist leads the viewer on a journey of noticing, appreciating, and identifying tiny, overlooked, and potentially unnamed funga.
