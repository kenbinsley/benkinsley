Ben Kinsley is an interdisciplinary artist working in sound, video, and social/contextual practice. His projects have ranged from choreographing a neighborhood intervention into Google Street View, directing surprise theatrical performances inside the homes of strangers, staging a royal protest, collecting put-down jokes from around the world, and hosting a lecture series in an aspen grove.

He also creates award winning mushroom costumes which have been featured in the mockumentary “Fest in Show”, the film “Fantastic Fungi”, and in Mushroom People Magazine, Vogue, and National Geographic.

He is an Associate Professor and Director of Visual Art in the Department of Visual & Performing Arts at CU Colorado Springs.
