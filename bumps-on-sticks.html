Bumps on Sticks

Video (2025)
HD | 3:56 | Subtitle: English

Meet genus Chlorociboria! These gorgeous blue-green ‘bumps on sticks’ pull you into the micro world of sac fungi. Characters, including three human-sized elf cups, a loupe for scale, and a mycologist, will guide you through the process of close observation, documentation, and proper specimen collection. You will leave ready to join the ranks of the global citizen-mycologist kindom.
